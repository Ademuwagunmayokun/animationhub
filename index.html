ABOUT ANIMATION HUB

Empowering Creators Through Innovation & African Stories

Animation Hub is a creative technology organization focused on empowering the next generation of African creators through animation, AI, digital arts, and storytelling.

Our aim is to empower creators through innovative animation, AI, and storytelling, while helping African stories reach the world. We combine artistic excellence with practical technology to help businesses and creators thrive.
